THE ONE TEST THAT SETTLES IT

Strategy Properties → set commission and slippage to zero → re-run the identical backtest.

PROFIT FACTOR > 1.0

The signal has an edge; cost is eating it. Do not touch the channel length — you would be tuning a working part. Move it to 30m–1h.

STILL < 1.0

The signal is unprofitable before costs. No timeframe or parameter set saves it; a higher timeframe only loses more slowly. Rebuild the premise.

WHAT WON'T HELP

  • Tuning the channel length. The deficit is 34% of payoff. A search on 1m fits these ten days, not the market.
  • More trades, or bigger size. Expectancy is negative per trade — volume and size scale the loss linearly.
  • A tighter stop. It raises the spread's share of every loss and pushes payoff further from 2.364×.
